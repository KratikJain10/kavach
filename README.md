# Kavach 🛡️

An offline, open-source scam-call guard for India.

Put a phone call on speaker next to your laptop. Kavach listens, transcribes the call live in Hindi, English
or Hinglish, and warns you mid-call when the caller uses a known scam script: fake police and "digital
arrest", KYC/OTP requests, parcels "with drugs", electricity cut-off threats, lottery prizes and more.

It runs entirely on your own device, so the call never leaves your computer. When you're online, Gemma 4
writes a short after-call report in simple Hindi explaining the trick and what to do next.

Built for Hacktoberfest Hack Day Mumbai.
